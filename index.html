About Gimme Insights

Insights that actually move the needle

Gimme Insights was founded in 2023 to be the insights partner businesses turn to when they need decisions grounded in both customer needs and commercial reality.

We don't just deliver reports. We diagnose business problems, select the right research methodology, and deliver high-quality insights that drive real decision making, whether that's launching a product, uncovering new growth opportunities, or building data capability inside your team.

Our work spans research, analytics, training, and go-to-market strategy, giving clients a single trusted partner across the full insights journey.

Meet the Founder

20 years turning customer insight into commercial wins

Catherine founded Gimme Insights in 2023 after 20 years of insights experience across the APAC region. She has worked with some of the most recognised companies and brands, helping them uncover what their customers actually want and translating that into growth.

Her career started at Nielsen and Kantar, where she built a strong foundation in diagnosing business problems and delivering high-quality insights. From there, she moved into loyalty, designing and measuring the commercial impact of promotions for major grocery retailers including Coles, Woolworths, Carrefour, PX Mart, and AEON.

Catherine is passionate about closing the gap between customers and the businesses that serve them, where great insight creates wins on both sides.
